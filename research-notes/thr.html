Activation thresholds by target

For each biochemical or physiological target: the reported or computed window in field strength (B), rate of change (dB/dt) and induced electric field (E), which of those the evidence says actually drives the response, and how far the numbers can be trusted. Every number is tagged reported (from the paper) or computed (this project, with stated assumptions). No study varies rise time or frequency at constant B with matched E, so most biochemical targets remain unresolved. Treat the windows as where effects were seen, not as proven thresholds.
